## Unit 2 — Research integrity / ethics body (the body the office supports)

1. **Official name of the unit**
   **Comitato Etico dell’Università degli Studi di Milano** ([Università degli Studi di Milano][2])

2. **Institutional location**
   **Not specified in official sources** as being located within a specific administrative department (it is established by/within the university’s governance-regulatory framework). ([Università degli Studi di Milano][2])

3. **Core responsibilities** (explicitly stated)

* **Handling academic misconduct cases:**

  * Provides opinions on **violations of the Codice Etico** that **are not also disciplinary offences**. ([Università degli Studi di Milano][2])
* **Promoting academic integrity:**

  * Promotes ethical awareness/education (seminars/courses/initiatives) and “favorisce lo sviluppo della consapevolezza etica.” ([Università degli Studi di Milano][2])
* **Overseeing integrity regulations / ordinance:**

  * Gives interpretations/opinions on the **Codice Etico** (pareri and application; formal procedure for non-disciplinary violations is described). ([Università degli Studi di Milano][3])

4. **Source references**

* *Regolamento di funzionamento del Comitato Etico* (functions, scope incl. integrity of research; violations of Codice Etico). ([Università degli Studi di Milano][2])
* *Codice etico e per l’integrità nella ricerca (2019)* (education/promotion; procedure and role of Comitato etico for non-disciplinary violations). ([Università degli Studi di Milano][3])
* Governance page: *Comitato etico* (description and remit overview). ([Università degli Studi di Milano][4])
* *Statuto* (Art. 32 on Comitato etico: opinions on research with ethical implications + promoting awareness). ([Università degli Studi di Milano][5])

## Unit 3 — Academic misconduct (disciplinary) for professors/researchers

1. **Official name of the unit**
   **Collegio di disciplina** ([Università degli Studi di Milano][5])

2. **Institutional location**
   **Not specified in official sources** as belonging to a specific administrative department (it is constituted under the university Statute; governance-level body). ([Università degli Studi di Milano][5])

3. **Core responsibilities** (explicitly stated)

* **Handling academic misconduct cases:**

  * Competent to conduct the **investigatory phase** and issue a **concluding opinion** in disciplinary proceedings for **professors and researchers** (acts/behaviours unlawful or contrary to academic duties and university rules). ([Università degli Studi di Milano][5])
* **Promoting academic integrity:** Not specified in official sources. ([Università degli Studi di Milano][5])
* **Overseeing integrity regulations / ordinance:** Not specified in official sources (role is disciplinary procedure). ([Università degli Studi di Milano][5])

4. **Source references**

* *Statuto* (Art. 33: competence and disciplinary procedure flow involving Rettore/CdA). ([Università degli Studi di Milano][5])

## Unit 4 — Academic misconduct (disciplinary) for TAB staff

1. **Official name of the unit**
   **Ufficio per i Procedimenti Disciplinari (UPD)** ([Università degli Studi di Milano][6])

* **Promoting academic integrity:** Not specified in official sources. ([Università degli Studi di Milano][6])
* **Overseeing integrity regulations / ordinance:** Not specified in official sources (the page frames its remit as disciplinary procedure under D.Lgs. 165/2001). ([Università degli Studi di Milano][6])

4. **Source references**

## B) **Comitato etico** *(research ethics + ethical code violations that are not disciplinary)*

a. **Reports to**

* **Not specified in official sources** (as a formal reporting line).
* In the **Ethics Code procedure**, the Committee **transmits its (non-binding) opinion to the Rector (Rettore)**. ([Università degli Studi di Milano Statale][3])

b. **Decision-making authority**

* **Advisory (explicit):** the Committee issues a **“parere … non vincolante”** in the procedure for “comportamenti eticamente scorretti”. ([Università degli Studi di Milano Statale][3])
* **Final decision for non-disciplinary Ethics Code violations (explicit):** the **Senato accademico** decides on Ethics Code violations **not under the Collegio di disciplina**, **on proposal of the Rector**. ([Università degli Studi di Milano Statale][4])

c. **Appeal or second-opinion mechanisms**

* In the Ethics Code procedure, after the Committee’s opinion, **interested persons have 60 days to submit observations to the Rector**. ([Università degli Studi di Milano Statale][3])
* No further appeal mechanism is explicitly described in the cited official sources. **Not specified in official sources.**

d. **Source references**

* *Codice etico e per l’integrità nella ricerca* (esp. Art. 44–46: transmission to Committee; “parere … non vincolante”; 60-day observations to Rector). ([Università degli Studi di Milano Statale][3])
* *Statuto d’Ateneo* (Ethics Code violations decided by Senato accademico on Rector’s proposal when not under Collegio di disciplina). ([Università degli Studi di Milano Statale][4])
* *Regolamento di funzionamento del Comitato Etico* (Committee “esprime pareri…”, including on violations of the Code that are not disciplinary; appointment details). ([Università degli Studi di Milano Statale][5])

## C) **Collegio di disciplina** *(disciplinary proceedings for professors and researchers)*

a. **Reports to**

* **Not specified in official sources** (as a formal reporting line).
* Procedurally, the Collegio **transmits its “parere … vincolante” to the Consiglio di amministrazione**. ([Università degli Studi di Milano Statale][4])

b. **Decision-making authority**

* **Mixed (explicit):**

  * The **Rector** sends a motivated sanction proposal to the Collegio. ([Università degli Studi di Milano Statale][4])
  * The **Collegio** issues a **binding** reasoned opinion (“parere motivato e vincolante”) on both facts and sanction, and transmits it to the **Consiglio di amministrazione**. ([Università degli Studi di Milano Statale][4])
  * The **Consiglio di amministrazione** **imposes the sanction or orders archiving**, and the Rector must execute it. ([Università degli Studi di Milano Statale][4])

c. **Appeal or second-opinion mechanisms**

* The General Regulation describes an internal **recusal (ricusazione) request** mechanism: a request is decided **definitively** by the President of the Collegio (or by the Collegio if the President is challenged), and that decision is stated as **not challengeable** (“non è impugnabile”). ([Università degli Studi di Milano Statale][6])
* Broader appeal routes against the final disciplinary sanction: **Not specified in official sources** (in the cited materials).

d. **Source references**

* *Statuto d’Ateneo* (Art. 33: binding opinion; sanction imposed by Consiglio di amministrazione; execution by Rector). ([Università degli Studi di Milano Statale][4])
* *Regolamento generale d’Ateneo* (recusal/ricusazione mechanism and “non è impugnabile”). ([Università degli Studi di Milano Statale][6])

---

## D) **Ufficio Procedimenti Disciplinari (UPD)** *(disciplinary proceedings for TAB staff / equivalent)*

a. **Reports to**

* The UPD **“fa parte di” Avvocatura** (organisational placement). ([Università degli Studi di Milano Statale][7])
* The UPD is chaired by the **Direttore Generale pro tempore** “con funzioni di Presidente” (composition as published). ([Università degli Studi di Milano Statale][7])

b. **Decision-making authority**

* The UPD is explicitly described as responsible to **start, investigate, and conclude disciplinary proceedings** for sanctions above verbal reprimand, and to conclude with **issuance of the (possible) disciplinary measure** (“emissione dell’eventuale provvedimento disciplinare”). ([Università degli Studi di Milano Statale][7])
* Whether any other UniMI body must formally approve/ratify the UPD’s disciplinary measure: **Not specified in official sources** (in the cited UPD page/decree).

c. **Appeal or second-opinion mechanisms**

* **Not specified in official sources.**

d. **Source references**

* UniMI organisational page: *Ufficio Procedimenti Disciplinari* (parent unit “Avvocatura”, mandate, activities, composition). ([Università degli Studi di Milano Statale][7])
* UniMI decree PDF on UPD composition (mentions Direzione Generale role and organisational reconfiguration including Avvocatura). ([Università degli Studi di Milano Statale][8])
