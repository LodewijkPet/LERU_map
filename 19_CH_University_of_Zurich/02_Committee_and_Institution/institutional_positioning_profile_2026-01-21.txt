I’ll look up the University of Zurich’s official research integrity structures (policies + responsible offices), then extract only what’s explicitly stated about the unit name, where it sits in the organization, and what it does—citing the exact official sources.


1. **Official name of the unit**
   Research Integrity Coordination Office *(Geschäftsstelle Wissenschaftliche Integrität)*. ([research.uzh.ch][1])

2. **Institutional location**
   Located within the **Office of the Vice President Research** *(Prorektorat Forschung)*, shown as part of the **Research & Grants Office**. ([research.uzh.ch][1])

3. **Core responsibilities**

* **Handling academic misconduct cases** *(explicitly mentioned)*

  * The **Office of the Vice President Research** is responsible for “reports and investigations” in academic integrity under the Integrity Ordinance framework. ([rud.uzh.ch][2])
  * The **ombudspersons** are the “first and central point of contact” and **receive reports** of suspected scientific misconduct. ([research.uzh.ch][3])
  * The **Research Integrity Delegates** conduct procedures to investigate suspected misconduct on behalf of the Executive Board. ([research.uzh.ch][3])
  * The process is **initiated by the Research Integrity Delegate (or deputy) and the Research Integrity Coordination Office**. ([rud.uzh.ch][2])

* **Promoting academic integrity** *(explicitly mentioned)*

  * The **Research Integrity Coordination Office** provides **information and training** to promote academic integrity and prevent misconduct. ([openscience.uzh.ch][4])

* **Overseeing integrity regulations / ordinance**

  * **Not specified in official sources.** (The Integrity Ordinance is referenced, but an explicit “oversight” mandate for this unit is not stated.) ([rud.uzh.ch][2])

5. **Reporting & authority structure**

a. **Reports to**

* **Research Integrity Coordination Office (Geschäftsstelle Wissenschaftliche Integrität):** *Not specified in official sources* (no explicit “reports to …” line found for this office). Its institutional placement is shown under the **Office of the Vice President Research / Prorektorat Forschung → Research & Grants Office**, but a formal reporting statement is not spelled out. ([research.uzh.ch][1])
* **Within the misconduct procedure (explicit reporting / information flow):**

  * The **ombudsperson** informs the **University’s Executive Board (Universitätsleitung)** if they consider a formal procedure necessary. ([research.uzh.ch][2])
  * The **Research Integrity Delegate / Integritätsbeauftragte*r** conducts the investigation **on behalf of** the University’s Executive Board, and closes the procedure with a **written report addressed to the Universitätsleitung**. ([research.uzh.ch][2])

b. **Decision-making authority**

* **Final decision authority (explicit):** The **University’s Executive Board (Universitätsleitung)** is explicitly named as the **decision-making body** and **decides** on (i) whether misconduct occurred and (ii) measures to be taken (or recommended). ([research.uzh.ch][2])

c. **Appeal or second-opinion mechanisms**

* **Internal legal appeal (explicit):** The accused person can file an appeal (**Rekurs**) against decisions (**Verfügungen**) under the Integrity Ordinance **within 30 days** to the **Rekurskommission der Zürcher Hochschulen**, under the **Verwaltungsrechtspflegegesetz**. ([Notes][3])

5. **Reporting & authority structure**

a. **Reports to**

* **Integrity officer / investigation body → University Executive Board**: The *Integritätsbeauftragte/r* (or an *Untersuchungskommission*) closes the investigation with a **written report addressed to the Universitätsleitung** (“Bericht zuhanden der Universitätsleitung”). ([UZH News][1])
* **Trusted person → University Executive Board (and Legal Service)**: If the *Vertrauensperson* considers that a formal procedure should be opened, they **inform the Universitätsleitung**, the **Rechtsdienst**, and the accused person. ([UZH News][1])
* **Research Integrity Coordination Office (Geschäftsstelle Wissenschaftliche Integrität)**: **Not specified in official sources** as a formal “reports to” line (i.e., no explicit statement of reporting/line management). The office is shown organisationally under *Prorektorat Forschung* on UZH pages. ([research.uzh.ch][2])

b. **Decision-making authority**

* **Final decision authority (explicit): Universitätsleitung**

  * The Universitätsleitung is explicitly described as the **decision-making instance** and **decides on the existence of scientific misconduct** and **orders or recommends measures**. ([uzh.ch][3])
* **Advisory / investigative roles (explicit):**

  * *Vertrauensperson* = advisory/support/mediation + preliminary assessment/pre-checks. ([UZH News][1])
  * *Integritätsbeauftragte/r* (or *Untersuchungskommission*) = conducts the procedure/investigation and submits findings/recommendations to the Universitätsleitung. ([UZH News][1])

c. **Appeal or second-opinion mechanisms**

* **Appeal (explicit):** The accused person may file a **Rekurs** against decisions (“Verfügungen”) based on the Integrity Ordinance to the **Rekurskommission der Zürcher Hochschulen** within **30 days** of receipt (per the administrative procedure law of the Canton of Zurich). ([UZH News][1])
* **Internal hearing request (explicit):** The accused person may request an **oral hearing by the Universitätsleitung**. ([UZH News][1])
