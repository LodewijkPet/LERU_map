## University of Edinburgh (UK) — Research integrity / research misconduct (research activities)

1. **Official name of the unit**
   **Research Governance, Compliance & Risk team** (within the **Edinburgh Research Office (ERO)**). ([Edinburgh Research Office][1])

2. **Institutional location**
   Located in the **Edinburgh Research Office (ERO)** (described under the ERO “About us” structure and explicitly referenced as “within the Edinburgh Research Office”). ([Edinburgh Research Office][1])

3. **Core responsibilities**

* **Handling academic misconduct cases:**

  * **Explicitly mentioned (for research misconduct allegations):** “Named Persons within each college handle allegations of research misconduct, overseeing and managing investigations at the college level.” 
  * The **Vice-Principal Research and Enterprise** is stated as the “main responsible contact for Research Misconduct” (with responsibility delegated in each College). ([Edinburgh Research Office][2])
* **Promoting academic integrity:**

  * **Explicitly mentioned:** ERO’s Research Governance, Compliance & Risk team states it collaborates to “promote and embed a culture of good practice in research” and enables researchers to meet responsibilities relating to “research integrity and ethics.” ([Edinburgh Research Office][1])
  * A Research Integrity and Ethics Officer role description explicitly includes supporting “the promotion of research culture, research integrity and good research practice.” ([EdWeb Profiles][3])
* **Overseeing integrity regulations / ordinance:**

  * **Explicitly mentioned:** The **Edinburgh Research Office** “oversees key policies, including the Ethics Policy and the Research Misconduct Policy,” and the **Research Governance, Compliance & Risk team** ensures these policies are revised to align with the Concordat and UKRIO guidance. 

4. **Source references**

* Edinburgh Research Office — “Research misconduct” page. ([Edinburgh Research Office][2])
* Edinburgh Research Office — “About us” (describes Research Governance, Compliance & Risk and its remit). ([Edinburgh Research Office][1])
* **UoE Annual Research Integrity Statement 2023–2024** (PDF, hosted by Edinburgh Research Office). 
* University profile page for “Research Integrity and Ethics Officer” (shows ERO + Research Governance, Compliance & Risk team placement and remit). ([EdWeb Profiles][3])

## Student academic misconduct (assessed work) and escalation into student discipline

**a. Reports to**
Not specified in official sources (for Academic Misconduct Officers/panels). ([cahss.ed.ac.uk][4])

For **student discipline under the Code of Student Conduct**:

* The Code states students are subject to the **jurisdiction of the Senate**, and that **“The Senate has primary responsibility for student discipline and recommends to the University Court the University’s disciplinary procedure.”** 
* The Code also states the lead Secretary **“informs the relevant Senate committee annually”** of all cases considered by Student Discipline Officers / the Student Discipline Committee. 

**b. Decision-making authority**

* **College Academic Misconduct Officer (CAMO):** explicitly stated to **investigate**, **determine whether there has been an offence**, and determine whether a **penalty should be applied** or whether the offence is serious enough to proceed to a **disciplinary hearing**. ([cahss.ed.ac.uk][4])
* **Under the Code of Student Conduct:** the processes “task various members of the University community” with **investigating, determining and imposing penalties**; decisions are made by roles including Conduct Investigators, Student Discipline Officers, and (where applicable) the Student Discipline Committee. 
* The Code states that the **University Appeal Committee’s decision is final within the University** (after an appeal). 

**c. Appeal or second-opinion mechanisms**

* **Internal appeal (discipline):** If an allegation is upheld, the respondent may appeal within **10 working days** to the **Secretary of the University’s Appeal Committee**; **grounds for appeal are specified in the Student Appeal Regulations**; the appeal is handled under University appeal procedures; **the Appeal Committee decision is final within the University**. 
* **External review (after appeal):** After the appeal is completed, the respondent may ask the **Scottish Public Services Ombudsman (SPSO)** to look at their appeal. 
* For **academic misconduct cases that CAMO refers to a disciplinary hearing**, the above Code-of-Student-Conduct appeal route is the explicitly documented appeal route available for the discipline decision-makers. ([cahss.ed.ac.uk][4])

**d. Source references**

* CAHSS page: “Academic misconduct” (roles, CAMO authority, panel composition) ([cahss.ed.ac.uk][4])
* University Court papers (includes the Code of Student Conduct text with authority + appeals + reporting/recording)
