## 4. Report scope

The report should answer five questions:

1. How are research-integrity responsibilities allocated across national bodies, institutions, funders, academies and specialist routes?
2. Where can a concern enter, travel, be decided, reviewed and—where appropriate—be reported publicly?
3. How do procedure visibility, aggregate activity reporting and case-level public output differ?
4. What does the non-ranking 24-member LERU comparison show, and what remains unvalidated?
5. What minimum route, reporting and maintenance practices could LERU and member universities adopt without compromising confidentiality?

The main text should remain a focused 30-40 page advice paper or public report. Detailed country material, the full member matrix, codebook, validation questions, source protocol and reproducibility material belong in the technical annex. Do not turn the report into 49 country summaries.
