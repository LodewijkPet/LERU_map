5. Reporting & authority structure (UCL)

a. Reports to
**Research misconduct (staff / research students, “misconduct in academic research”)**

* **Research Misconduct Committee (RMC):** submits minutes to **UCL’s Research, Innovation and Global Engagement Committee (RIGEC)** and produces an annual report for onward reporting to **RIGEC** and **UCL’s Audit Committee**. ([University College London][1])
* **Named Person (UCL Registrar / interim Named Person):** the Procedure’s **annual review** mechanism states the Registrar will submit annually to **Audit Committee** and **RIGEC** a copy of the Procedure and a report on significant issues arising in its operation. ([University College London][2])
* **Where RIGEC sits in UCL governance:** UCL’s committees chart places **RIGEC** within the **UCL University Management Committee (UMC)** committee structure. 

**Student academic misconduct (summative assessment misconduct)**

* **Academic Misconduct Appeals Panel:** the Academic Manual states the Appeals Panel decision is included in an **annual report on its proceedings submitted to the UCL Education Committee**. ([University College London][3])
* **Academic Misconduct Panel (first-instance):** reporting line **not specified in official sources** (the documents describe panel composition and decision process, but do not state a formal reporting line). ([University College London][3])

b. Decision-making authority
**Research misconduct**

* **Named Person (Registrar / interim Named Person):** explicitly responsible for “**taking decisions at key stages of the Procedure**” (and for initiating/supervising the Procedure). ([University College London][2])
* **RMC / Screening Panels:** screening stage determines whether there is **prima facie evidence** of misconduct in research (i.e., whether the matter proceeds). ([University College London][2])
* **Investigation Panel:** explicitly empowered to **reach conclusions** on allegations (upheld / partly upheld / not upheld) on the **balance of probabilities**, and submit a final report to the Registrar. ([University College London][2])
* **Disciplinary sanctions:** the Procedure states that disciplinary sanctions can **only** be applied through UCL’s **disciplinary processes** (i.e., the research misconduct procedure produces a report that may lead to disciplinary action). ([University College London][2])

**Student academic misconduct**

* **Academic Misconduct Panel:** explicitly “decide[s]… whether Academic Misconduct has occurred and, if so, determine[s] which penalty to impose.” ([University College London][4])
* **Appeals authority:** the Academic Manual states the **Chair of the Academic Misconduct Appeals Panel** may uphold, overturn, or modify the penalty; and an **Appeals Panel has the power to reverse or modify** the decision appealed. ([University College London][3])

c. Appeal or second-opinion mechanisms
**Research misconduct**

* The Procedure explicitly states that if disciplinary processes are invoked, those processes “**provide the Respondent with the right of appeal**.” The appeal body/mechanism is **not specified in official sources** within the Procedure itself. ([University College London][2])

**Student academic misconduct**

* **Internal appeal:** students may appeal Academic Misconduct Panel decisions on defined grounds (procedure not followed, fresh evidence, or unreasonable decision) and submit an appeal within the stated timeframe; appeals are handled via the Academic Misconduct Appeals process. ([University College London][5])
* **Final internal stage + external review:** the Academic Manual states an Appeals Panel decision is **final internally**, and then (after a Completion of Procedures letter) students may complain to the **Office of the Independent Adjudicator for Higher Education (OIA)**. ([University College London][3])
