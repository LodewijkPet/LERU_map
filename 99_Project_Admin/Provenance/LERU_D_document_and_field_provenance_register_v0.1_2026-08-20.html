Research-integrity concerns are handled at the interface of scientific norms, employment and disciplinary law, university governance, funder requirements, publication correction and national oversight. That interface is organised differently across Europe. In one university the visible front door may be a standing commission; elsewhere it is a rector, chancellor, named person, ombudsperson, scientific-integrity referent, research office or legal/governance team.
