## University of Helsinki – Finland

### A) Research integrity (alleged research misconduct in research)

1. **Official name of the unit**
   **Chancellor** ([University of Helsinki][1])

2. **Institutional location**
   **University-level authority: Chancellor.** (Further organisational placement is **not specified in official sources**.) ([University of Helsinki][1])

3. **Core responsibilities**

* **Handling academic misconduct cases:** **Explicitly mentioned** — the Chancellor “is in charge of inquiries concerning alleged violations of the research integrity,” and allegations must be submitted in writing to the Chancellor. ([University of Helsinki][1])
* **Promoting academic integrity:** **Not specified in official sources.** ([University of Helsinki][1])
* **Overseeing integrity regulations / ordinance:** **Not specified in official sources** (the page notes handling follows the relevant procedures/guidelines, but does not frame this as “oversight”). ([University of Helsinki][1])

### B) Research integrity support / prevention (non-adjudicative)

1. **Official name of the unit**
   **Research integrity advisers** (the **research integrity adviser system**) ([University of Helsinki][1])

2. **Institutional location**
   **Not specified in official sources.** ([University of Helsinki][1])

3. **Core responsibilities**

* **Handling academic misconduct cases:** **Explicitly not the case** — advisers “do not have authority in any RI processes… nor do they participate in them.” ([University of Helsinki][1])
* **Promoting academic integrity:** **Explicitly mentioned** — targeted at promoting research integrity, improving preventive measures, and providing confidential guidance/negotiation support on how to proceed. ([University of Helsinki][1])
* **Overseeing integrity regulations / ordinance:** **Not specified in official sources.** ([University of Helsinki][1])

### C) Academic misconduct in studies (e.g., fraudulent conduct; disciplinary procedure support)

1. **Official name of the unit**
   **Legal services for academic affairs** ([teaching.helsinki.fi][3])

2. **Institutional location**
   **Not specified in official sources.** ([teaching.helsinki.fi][3])

3. **Core responsibilities**

* **Handling academic misconduct cases:** **Explicitly mentioned (procedural role)** — in disciplinary procedures, the unit’s legal counsel “would then be responsible for preparing and presenting the matter.” ([teaching.helsinki.fi][3])
