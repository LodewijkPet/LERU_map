## University of Cambridge (UK)

### A) Research integrity / research misconduct

1. **Official name of the unit**
   Research Governance and Integrity Team ([University of Cambridge][1])

2. **Institutional location**

* “A team within the **University Research Office**…” ([University of Cambridge][1])
* Also described as being **based in the Cambridge Research Office**. ([University of Cambridge][2])
* Research-integrity queries are routed to the Pro-Vice-Chancellor for Research via the **Research Strategy Office** (contact routing; institutional “home” not further specified on that page). ([University of Cambridge][3])

3. **Core responsibilities** (only what is explicitly stated)

* **Handling academic misconduct cases (research misconduct): Explicitly mentioned**

  * Supports the administration/running of the University’s procedure for investigating allegations of research misconduct. ([University of Cambridge][1])
  * Designated to support the Responsible Person in handling investigations under the procedure. ([University of Cambridge][1])
* **Promoting academic integrity: Explicitly mentioned (via advice/training support functions)**

  * Provides administrative support and triage for the Research Integrity Advisory Panel; answers straightforward queries or routes them to a panel member. ([University of Cambridge][2])
  * Uses aggregated query information to help improve/develop training courses, resources and guidance. ([University of Cambridge][4])
* **Overseeing integrity regulations / ordinance: Explicitly mentioned (procedural oversight of the misconduct procedure)**

  * May propose procedural variations to the Secretary of the Process; keeps formal records of variations/decisions; advises on reporting requirements. ([University of Cambridge][1])

4. **Source references**

* “Research integrity” (contact points; PVCR routed via research governance & integrity team). ([University of Cambridge][5])
* “Research Integrity Advisory Panel” (team based in Cambridge Research Office; admin support/triage). ([University of Cambridge][2])
* “Research Integrity Advisory Panel procedures” (team part of Cambridge Research Office; query-handling; training/resources improvement). ([University of Cambridge][4])
* “Research integrity statement” (PVCR contacted through Research Strategy Office). ([University of Cambridge][3])
* “Procedure for the Investigation of an Allegation of Research Misconduct” (definitions + role of Research Governance and Integrity Team). ([University of Cambridge][1])

5. Reporting & authority structure (University of Cambridge – UK)

a. Reports to
**Research misconduct (research integrity procedure)**

* **Not specified in official sources** (no explicit “reports to” line for the *Research Governance and Integrity Team*).
* The Procedure states that the **Secretary of the Process** (defined as the **Academic Secretary or the Registrary**) oversees application of the procedure, and that the **Responsible Person** (the **Head of the relevant University Institution** or the **Pro-Vice-Chancellor with responsibility for Research**) considers the Stage 3 report “taking the advice of the Secretary of the Process and the Research Governance and Integrity Team.” ([University of Cambridge][1])
* Where serious systemic issues about research integrity are identified, the Procedure states the **Research Governance and Integrity Team will inform the Pro-Vice-Chancellor with responsibility for Research.** ([University of Cambridge][1])

**Student academic misconduct (student disciplinary procedure / OSCCA)**

* The Student Disciplinary Procedure states that **OSCCA produces an annual report** which “shall be submitted to the **General Board** (through its **Education Committee**) and to the **Council**.” 

b. Decision-making authority
**Research misconduct (research integrity procedure)**

* The Procedure states the **Responsible Person** considers the Stage 3 report and takes actions “as they deem appropriate,” including determinations that (i) misconduct did not occur (dismiss), (ii) misconduct occurred and is referred to disciplinary procedures, or other specified outcomes. ([University of Cambridge][1])
* The **Appeal Manager** (appointed by the Academic Secretary) decides the appeal outcome (uphold/dismiss) and may, where appropriate, refer the case back for reconsideration. ([University of Cambridge][1])

**Student academic misconduct (student disciplinary procedure / OSCCA)**

* The glossary states the **Student Discipline Officer (SDO)** is “appointed by Grace” and has authority to impose sanctions. 
* The glossary states the **Discipline Committee** (appointed by the General Board) determines whether Rules were breached and “has the authority to impose sanctions or measures.” 
* The glossary states the **Appeal Committee** determines appeals and has authority “to amend, quash or impose sanctions or measures.” 

c. Appeal or second-opinion mechanisms
**Research misconduct (research integrity procedure)**

* The Procedure provides an internal **appeal** stage decided by an **Appeal Manager** appointed by the Academic Secretary. ([University of Cambridge][1])
* It states that after the Appeal Manager’s decision there is **“no further right to appeal.”** ([University of Cambridge][1])

**Student academic misconduct (student disciplinary procedure / OSCCA)**

* The Procedure provides an internal **appeal to an Appeal Committee**, with appeal grounds and process described in the “Appeal Committee” section. 
* The glossary defines a **“Completion of Procedures letter”** and states that, after completion of internal procedures, a student “may be able to raise a complaint with the **Office of the Independent Adjudicator**.”
