I’ll pull Trinity College Dublin’s official policies and governance pages (including any regulations on research integrity/misconduct) and extract exactly where the responsible unit sits, then present it in your required 4-part structure with citations.


1. Official name of the unit
   **Office of the Dean of Research** (incl. the **Research Ethics and Integrity Administrator**). 
   (With the **Senior Dean** serving as the **College Research Integrity Officer (RIO)**.) 

2. Institutional location

* **Research integrity / research misconduct coordination:** located in the **Office of the Dean of Research** (the policy states this office coordinates preliminary enquiries and has the designated Research Ethics and Integrity Administrator for ethics & integrity matters). 
* **Institutional oversight role:** the **Senior Dean** is explicitly identified as the **College Research Integrity Officer (RIO)**. 
* **Where cases go (disciplinary route, if upheld):**

  * staff: **office of the Senior Dean** 
  * undergraduates: **office of the Junior Dean** 
  * postgraduates: **office of the Dean of Graduate Studies** 
* **Public concerns:** Trinity notes that “general concerns from the public” are referred to the **College Secretary’s office**. ([Trinity College Dublin][1])

3. Core responsibilities

* **Handling academic misconduct cases — explicitly mentioned**

  * The **RIO (Senior Dean)** is named, and the RIO **delegates the Dean of Research to manage allegations of Research Misconduct** and implement an investigation process. 
  * The **Office of the Dean of Research** coordinates **informal preliminary enquiries**; the RIO can instigate a **formal investigation** via the Dean of Research. 
  * The **Research Ethics and Integrity Administrator** (within the Office of the Dean of Research) is designated to manage/document allegations and support investigations. 
  * If allegations are deemed legitimate, **disciplinary proceedings** are addressed by: Senior Dean (staff) / Junior Dean (undergraduate) / Dean of Graduate Studies (postgraduate). 

* **Promoting academic integrity — explicitly mentioned**

  * Trinity’s policy states that **“Education and promotion of good research practice”** are foundations of research integrity (commitment to maintaining a culture of integrity and promoting good practice). 
  * Trinity provides access to **research integrity training (Epigeum)** and directs users to contact a person **in the Office of the Dean of Research** for access. ([Trinity College Dublin][2])
  * Trinity’s Academic Integrity page explicitly links **Research Integrity** support/guidance to **the office of the Dean of Research**. ([Trinity College Dublin][3])

* **Overseeing integrity regulations / ordinance — Not specified in official sources.**
  (The sources above specify case-handling roles/processes and training/support, but do not explicitly state that the Office of the Dean of Research “oversees” the integrity regulations/ordinance as an owner/authority.)

5. Reporting & authority structure

a. Reports to

* **Research misconduct / research integrity process:** The **Dean of Research** reports the **outcome of any investigation** to the **College Research Integrity Officer (RIO)** (the **Senior Dean**). 
* **Academic misconduct (student cases):** Not specified in official sources as a “formal reporting line,” but the **Academic Integrity Meeting** outcome is **submitted for approval** to the **Senior Lecturer/Dean of Undergraduate Studies** or the **Dean of Graduate Studies** (depending on the student type/case). 

b. Decision-making authority

* **Research misconduct / research integrity:**

  * The **RIO (Senior Dean)** is identified as the College Research Integrity Officer and **delegates the Dean of Research** to manage allegations and implement the investigation process; the **Dean of Research reports the investigation outcome** to the RIO. 
  * Where allegations are “deemed legitimate,” the **Senior Dean** addresses **disciplinary proceedings for staff**, and the **Junior Dean** (undergraduate) / **Dean of Graduate Studies** (postgraduate) addresses **student disciplinary proceedings**. 
  * Trinity’s Statutes also assign the **RIO decision authority** on specific matters (e.g., determining whether and how far disclosure to third parties is necessary where misconduct is upheld). 

* **Academic misconduct / student discipline:**

  * The **Senior Lecturer/Dean of Undergraduate Studies** or **Dean of Graduate Studies** may **approve, reject, or vary** the recommended consequence from the Academic Integrity Meeting (or seek further information). 
  * For student disciplinary offences handled “summarily,” the **Junior Dean** is explicitly the **final decision maker**, **unless appealed** to the **Student Conduct Committee**. 

c. Appeal or second-opinion mechanisms

* **Student disciplinary matters:**

  * **Appeal route:** Decisions of the **Junior Dean** can be **appealed to the Student Conduct Committee** (with a specified timeframe and notification route). 
  * A **decision of the Board** to approve a decision of the **Student Conduct Committee** may be **appealed to the Visitors**. 

* **Staff disciplinary matters (relevant where research misconduct proceeds under disciplinary procedures):**

  * The staff member is informed of the **right of appeal**; an **appeal must be lodged with the appropriate department head** within a specified timeframe, and **disciplinary action is not implemented pending appeal**. 

* **Research misconduct-specific second-opinion / external expertise mechanism:**

  * Trinity’s Statutes state the **RIO may seek assistance** from a **standing national panel of international experts** established by the **Royal Irish Academy** (or similar bodies). 
  * If an allegation is made **against the Senior Dean acting as RIO**, a **Pro-Senior Dean** is appointed to act as RIO for that allegation (a formal independence safeguard).
