Overall summary
Across the reviewed institutions, responsibility for research integrity case handling and integrity promotion is most often placed in a central governance unit (Rectorate, Executive Board, Vice-Chancellor/President) or within a research support office. In many systems, formal case handling sits with a committee or named officer, supported administratively by Legal Affairs or a research office. Several universities split responsibilities between research integrity (research office or Rectorate) and student academic misconduct (student services or disciplinary bodies). For a number of institutions, official sources do not specify a single administrative home for the research integrity function, so the location should be confirmed directly.

Per institution summary
ETH Zurich
- Unit(s): Scientific Integrity Office (supports Integrity Commission)
- Institutional location: Executive Board domain of Research
- Notes: Case handling supported by SIO; Integrity Commission conducts investigations

Heidelberg University
- Unit(s): Senate Commission for Safeguarding Good Academic Practice; Ombudspersons
- Institutional location: University governance (Senate/Rectorate); legal affairs listed as administrative contact
- Missing data: No explicit statement of a single administrative office location

Imperial College London
- Unit(s): Research Misconduct Review Group (RMRG); Research Governance and Integrity Team (RGIT)
- Institutional location: RMRG location not specified; RGIT is part of the Research Office
- Missing data: No official statement placing RMRG within a specific office

KU Leuven
- Unit(s): Research Integrity and Ethics unit; Research Integrity Reporting Desk; Commission on Research Integrity
- Institutional location: Research Coordination Office under Research Policy
- Notes: Reporting Desk is housed in the Research Coordination Office; CRI handles formal complaints

Leiden University
- Unit(s): Academic Integrity Committee
- Institutional location: University committee; secretariat in Legal Affairs
- Notes: Committee advises Executive Board

LMU Munich
- Unit(s): Ombudspersons for good scientific practice; Investigation Committee; Self-control officer
- Institutional location: University governance under Senate; no specific administrative office stated
- Missing data: No explicit administrative home beyond governance structures

Lund University
- Unit(s): Deviations from Good Research Practice Review Board
- Institutional location: Office of the Vice-Chancellor (University management)
- Notes: Board prepares decisions for the Vice-Chancellor

Paris-Saclay University
- Unit(s): POLAETHIS (research ethics and scientific integrity council) and network of scientific integrity referents
- Institutional location: Placed with the University Presidency
- Notes: Referents handle reports and promote integrity culture

Sorbonne University
- Unit(s): Delegation for scientific integrity led by the Scientific Integrity Referent
- Institutional location: Not specified in official sources; referent appointed by the President and reports to academic bodies
- Missing data: No explicit administrative home stated

Trinity College Dublin
- Unit(s): Office of the Dean of Research; Senior Dean as Research Integrity Officer
- Institutional location: Office of the Dean of Research; disciplinary routing via Senior Dean/Junior Dean/Dean of Graduate Studies
- Notes: Public concerns routed to the College Secretary

University College London
- Unit(s): Compliance and Assurance team (Research and Innovation Services); Governance Team (Office of General Counsel); Casework and Regulations Team (Student and Registry Services)
- Institutional location: RIS for integrity training/support; Governance Team for research misconduct procedure; Casework team in Student and Registry Services for student cases
- Notes: Multiple units depending on research vs student misconduct

University of Amsterdam
- Unit(s): Committee for Academic Integrity
- Institutional location: University-wide committee appointed by Executive Board
- Notes: Committee advises Executive Board; provides prevention-policy advice

University of Barcelona
- Unit(s): Research Integrity Committee
- Institutional location: Not specified; complaints enter via Vice-Rector for Research; committee reports to Rector
- Missing data: No single administrative office stated

University of Cambridge
- Unit(s): Research Governance and Integrity Team; Research Integrity Advisory Panel support; OSCCA for student conduct
- Institutional location: Research Governance and Integrity Team within University Research Office/Cambridge Research Office; OSCCA within Student Operations (Education Services)
- Notes: Research integrity queries routed via Research Strategy Office to Pro-Vice-Chancellor for Research

University of Edinburgh
- Unit(s): Research Governance, Compliance and Risk team (Edinburgh Research Office); Named Persons in Colleges; SAMOS/CAMOS for student misconduct
- Institutional location: Edinburgh Research Office for research integrity; student misconduct handled in colleges under Academic Services guidance
- Missing data: No single central office named for student misconduct handling

University of Freiburg
- Unit(s): Coordination Office for Research Integrity
- Institutional location: Established by Rectorate; staffed by adviser to Prorector for research integrity
- Notes: Handles case support, prevention measures, and development of integrity guidelines

University of Geneva
- Unit(s): Legal Affairs Service; Rectorate-led integrity procedure with faculty integrity delegates
- Institutional location: Legal Affairs attached to Rectorate; integrity procedures centered on Rectorate
- Notes: Legal Affairs listed as responsible for integrity directives

University of Helsinki
- Unit(s): Chancellor (research integrity inquiries); Research integrity advisers (prevention); Legal services for academic affairs (student discipline support)
- Institutional location: Chancellor is university-level authority; advisers and legal services location not specified
- Missing data: No explicit administrative placement for advisers or legal services

University of Milan
- Unit(s): Research Ethics Office; Ethics Committee; Collegio di disciplina; Disciplinary Proceedings Office (UPD)
- Institutional location: Ethics Office within staff unit for privacy and research ethics; Ethics Committee is governance-level; UPD in Legal Affairs
- Notes: Multiple bodies depending on ethics support vs disciplinary cases

University of Oxford
- Unit(s): Research Strategy and Policy Unit (Research Services); Registrar for formal allegations; Research and Innovation Committee for integrity policy
- Institutional location: Research Services for integrity support; Registrar is named officer for misconduct cases
- Notes: Student academic misconduct handled separately by Proctors (not within research integrity procedure)

University of Strasbourg
- Unit(s): Scientific Integrity Referent
- Institutional location: Not specified; referent reports to University President
- Missing data: No explicit administrative home stated

University of Zurich
- Unit(s): Research Integrity Coordination Office; ombudspersons and research integrity delegates
- Institutional location: Office of the Vice President Research within the Research and Grants Office
- Notes: Coordination Office initiates process with integrity delegate

University of Copenhagen
- Unit(s): Committee for Responsible Research Practice
- Institutional location: Appointed by Rector; secretariat serviced by Research and Information Security in University Administration
- Notes: Committee handles questionable research practice and refers misconduct to national board

Utrecht University
- Unit(s): Committee for Research Integrity
- Institutional location: Established by Executive Board; supported by Legal Affairs
- Notes: Committee advises Executive Board on cases
