1. **Official name of the unit**
   **Committee for Responsible Research Practice (the Practice Committee)** 

2. **Institutional location**

* **Set up/appointed by:** **the Rector** (UCPH) 
* **Secretariat / administrative servicing:** the Practice Committee “is serviced by” **UCPH Research & Information Security**, which is a **group unit in the University Administration**. ([praksisudvalget.ku.dk][1])

3. **Core responsibilities**

* **Handling academic misconduct cases (research misconduct / questionable research practice): *Explicitly mentioned***

  * Assesses whether complaints concern **questionable research practice** or **research misconduct**; examines complaints and issues statements in cases of questionable research practice. ([praksisudvalget.ku.dk][2])
  * **Refers/forwards cases involving research misconduct** to the **Danish Committee/Board on Research Misconduct**. ([praksisudvalget.ku.dk][2])

* **Promoting academic integrity / responsible conduct of research: *Explicitly mentioned***

  * Helps **clarify norms** for responsible research practice and **ensures norms are discussed** (incl. hosting conferences on responsible conduct of research). ([praksisudvalget.ku.dk][3])

* **Overseeing integrity regulations / ordinance: *Explicitly mentioned (in the sense of proposing rules/guidance)***

  * May **propose rules and guidance** on responsible research practice (mandated by the Rector or on its own initiative).

5. **Reporting & authority structure**

a. **Reports to**

* **Rector (UCPH)**: the Practice Committee is **appointed by the Rector**; the **Rector provides secretarial services**; and the committee **forwards its statement to the Rector** (and also submits reports to the Rector “when necessary”). ([about.ku.dk][1])
* **Independence caveat (explicit):** in the assessment of specific cases, the Practice Committee is **independent of the Rector** and “not under the Rector’s authority” in those contexts. 

b. **Decision-making authority**

* **Explicitly stated authority:**

  * The Practice Committee **terminates its proceedings with a “statement of opinion”** on whether a case can be characterised as **questionable research practice**. 
  * It can **reject** a case of questionable research practice if manifestly unfounded / outside remit. 
  * It **forwards** its statement to the parties and the **Rector “for information and any further action.”** 
* **Research misconduct cases:** if the claim concerns **research misconduct**, the Practice Committee **sends the case to the Danish Committee on Research Misconduct** (subject to conditions described in the rules). 
* **Sanctions / final disciplinary decisions:** **Not specified in official sources.** (The rules state the Rector may take “any further action,” but do not explicitly describe the committee as having sanctioning authority.) 

c. **Appeal or second-opinion mechanisms**

* **No administrative appeal (explicit):** the Practice Committee’s **statements of opinion cannot be appealed to any other administrative authority.** 
* **Reopening mechanism (explicit):** cases can **only be reopened** if new information emerges that presumably would have led to a different outcome (with additional conditions set out in the rules). 
* **Appeal options at the national body (Danish Committee on Research Misconduct):** **Not specified in official sources** (in the UCPH documents/pages used here).
