I’ll look up the University of Amsterdam’s official research integrity/misconduct structures on UvA’s own sites and governance documents, then extract only what’s explicitly stated and present it in your requested 4-part format with sources.


1. **Official name of the unit**
   **Commissie wetenschappelijke integriteit (UvA)** ([Universiteit van Amsterdam][1])

2. **Institutional location**
   **University-wide committee appointed by the Executive Board (College van Bestuur, CvB)**, and it **issues advice to the CvB** as part of the formal complaints procedure. 

3. **Core responsibilities**

* **Handling academic misconduct cases** — **Explicitly mentioned**

  * Receives formal complaints about suspected breaches of scientific/academic integrity. ([Universiteit van Amsterdam][1])
  * Assesses admissibility (“ontvankelijkheid”) and conducts an investigation (collects information, can hear witnesses/experts, speaks with those involved). ([Universiteit van Amsterdam][1])
  * Submits its advice to the Executive Board (CvB), after which the CvB gives its judgment. ([Universiteit van Amsterdam][1])

* **Promoting academic integrity** — **Explicitly mentioned (as prevention-policy advice)**

  * Provides the Executive Board with **solicited and unsolicited advice on the university’s prevention policy** regarding violations of academic integrity. 

* **Overseeing integrity regulations / ordinance** — **Not specified in official sources.**

5. **Reporting & authority structure**

a. **Reports to**

* **Executive Board (College van Bestuur / Executive Board)** — the Academic Integrity Committee “subsequently submits its advice to the Executive Board” and provides annual accountability reporting to the Executive Board. 
* **Supervisory Board (Raad van Toezicht / Supervisory Board)** — **only if** the complaint concerns a member of the Executive Board; in that case the Committee submits its advice to the Supervisory Board. 

b. **Decision-making authority**

* **Academic Integrity Committee:** **Advisory** (investigates and issues advice/recommendation). 
* **Executive Board:** **Final decision authority** (renders an **initial judgement** and—depending on whether LOWI advice is requested—makes the **final judgement**). 
* **Supervisory Board:** **Final decision authority in the specific case** where the complaint concerns a member of the Executive Board (it “will exercise the authorities pursuant to Article 11”). 

c. **Appeal or second-opinion mechanisms**

* **Second opinion via LOWI (National Board for Research Integrity):** the complainant and the accused **may ask LOWI for advice on the Executive Board’s initial judgement** within six weeks; the Executive Board then takes LOWI’s advice into consideration in its final judgement. 
* **Internal appeal:** **Not specified in official sources.**
