* **Untersuchungsausschuss** (als *ständiger Untersuchungsausschuss*)

2. Institutional location

* **University-level / central governance (not placed in a specific administrative department in the official sources).**

  * **Ombudspersonen**: appointed by the **Senate**; perform their role **independently**; receive support from the **university leadership (Leitung der LMU)**. No placement under a specific administrative office (e.g., Legal Affairs / Research Office) is stated. 
  * **Beauftragter/Beauftragte für die Selbstkontrolle in der Wissenschaft**: appointed by the **Senate** and listed by LMU under **“Organisation → Vertretungen und Beauftragte”**. No further departmental embedding is specified. ([LMU München][1])
  * **Untersuchungsausschuss**: established in the **Grundordnung** as a **standing committee (Ständiger Ausschuss)**; responsible for formal investigations and for preparing a recommendation to the **Hochschulleitung**. The procedure details are regulated in **guidelines issued by the Senate**. 

3. Core responsibilities

* **Handling academic misconduct cases — explicitly mentioned (research/scientific misconduct):**

  * **Ombudspersonen**: neutral contact point for suspected scientific misconduct; suspected cases should be reported to them; they can conduct **preliminary inquiries** and **preliminary review**, and may either **close** the matter or **transfer** it to a formal investigation. 
  * **Untersuchungsausschuss**: conducts the **formal investigation**; determines (by its own assessment) whether scientific misconduct is established; submits a **final report and recommendation** to the **Hochschulleitung**. 
  * **Hochschulleitung** (decision stage): if misconduct is established, the university leadership decides on **sanctions/measures** based on the committee’s report and recommendation. 
  * **Exam-/study-related misconduct**: LMU’s GWP regulation notes that *prüfungsrechtliches Fehlverhalten* is investigated by the **responsible bodies of the affected faculty** under examination regulations. 

* **Promoting academic integrity — partially / limited explicit mention:**

  * **Explicit:** Ombudspersonen provide **advice on questions of good scientific practice** (and support conflict mediation where possible). 
  * **Not specified in official sources:** a dedicated mandate for training/programmatic promotion beyond advisory support.

* **Overseeing integrity regulations / ordinance — not specified as a unit responsibility:**

  * **Explicit (governance of the procedure):** procedure details are set in **Senate-issued guidelines** (per Grundordnung); the Ombud and committee operate under the GWP regulation framework. 
  * **Not specified in official sources:** that the Ombud unit or Investigation Committee “oversees” the ordinance as an administrative owner.

## 3. Core responsibilities

### Handling academic misconduct / research misconduct cases (explicitly mentioned?)

* **Yes (explicit):**

  * The **Untersuchungsausschuss** conducts the **formal investigation** of allegations of *wissenschaftliches Fehlverhalten* and prepares a recommendation for the university leadership. ([stuve.uni-muenchen.de][1])
  * The **Ombudsperson(en)** advise in **suspected cases** of scientific misconduct and can be contacted by anyone at LMU. 

### Promoting academic integrity / good scientific practice (explicitly mentioned?)

* **Yes (explicit, via advisory role):**

  * The **Ombudsperson(en)** are “neutral and qualified contact persons” for questions of **good scientific practice** and contribute to conflict mediation. 
* **Not specified in official sources** (as a separate “promotion/training” mandate for the **Untersuchungsausschuss**). ([stuve.uni-muenchen.de][1])

### Overseeing integrity regulations / ordinance (explicitly mentioned?)

* **Not specified in official sources.** (The documents describe investigation/advice roles and procedural governance; they do not explicitly assign a general “oversight of the ordinance” function to these bodies.)

## 5. Reporting & authority structure

### a. Reports to

* **Untersuchungsausschuss → Hochschulleitung (University leadership):** submits an **“Abschlussbericht”** and **recommendation** for further action to the **Hochschulleitung**. 
* **Ombudsperson(en):** **Not specified in official sources** as a formal reporting line. (They are appointed by the Senate and act independently; the order does not describe a formal “reports to X” structure.) 

### b. Decision-making authority

* **Untersuchungsausschuss (explicit):** determines in the formal investigation whether *wissenschaftliches Fehlverhalten* is established (majority decision). 
* **Hochschulleitung (explicit):** decides **which sanctions/measures** are taken, based on the committee’s report and recommendation. 
* **Faculty (explicit, specific case):** if withdrawal of an academic degree is considered, the **responsible faculty** decides on degree withdrawal. 
* **Ombudsperson(en):** described as **advisory/mediation** (neutral contact persons; conflict mediation). **No final decision authority is specified** for them. 

### c. Appeal or second-opinion mechanisms

* **Internal appeal/complaint against the Untersuchungsausschuss decision:** explicitly **not available** (“Ein internes Beschwerdeverfahren … findet nicht statt.”). 
* **If the procedure is discontinued:** the complainant has **no remonstration** (“keine Remonstration”). 
* **External/alternative route (explicit):** instead of the LMU Ombudsperson, it is explicitly possible to contact the **“überregional tätige Ombudsgremium für die wissenschaftliche Integrität in Deutschland.”**
