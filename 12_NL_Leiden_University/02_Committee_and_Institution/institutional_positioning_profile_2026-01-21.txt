5. **Reporting & authority structure**

a. **Reports to**

* **Executive Board (College van Bestuur)** — the committee “submits [its] advice … to the Executive Board.” ([organisatiegids.universiteitleiden.nl][1])
* The complaints regulation also states that the committee “will report on its activities to the Executive Board by means of an annual report.” ([staff.universiteitleiden.nl][2])

b. **Decision-making authority**

* **Advisory (committee):** the committee “formulates its advice … and submits it to the Executive Board.” ([organisatiegids.universiteitleiden.nl][1])
* **Final decision authority (Executive Board):** the “Executive Board then decides whether to adopt the advice and, if the complaint is upheld, which measures will be taken,” and after the LOWI procedure the Executive Board “takes a final decision on the complaint.” ([organisatiegids.universiteitleiden.nl][1])

c. **Appeal or second-opinion mechanisms**

* **Second opinion:** a decision “can be submitted for a second opinion to the Netherlands Board on Research Integrity (LOWI), which then reviews the complaint and the procedure followed.” After that, the Executive Board takes the final decision. ([organisatiegids.universiteitleiden.nl][1])
* The university also describes decisions as “provisional” because parties have **six weeks** to request a second opinion from LOWI. ([Universiteit Leiden][3])
