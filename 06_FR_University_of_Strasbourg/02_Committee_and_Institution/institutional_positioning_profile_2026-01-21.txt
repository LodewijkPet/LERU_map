3. **Core responsibilities**

* **Handling academic misconduct cases (explicitly mentioned for research integrity / “intégrité scientifique”):**

  * Receives and processes reports (“signalements”) of possible breaches of research integrity. ([unistra.fr][1])
  * Verifies the report, may open an investigation/instruction, can seek expert advice, conducts a “contradictoire” (adversarial) process, and writes a report with recommendations submitted to the President/Presidenta. ([unistra.fr][1])
  * Examples of issues listed include plagiarism, inappropriate manipulation/fabrication of data, authorship conflicts, undisclosed conflicts of interest. ([unistra.fr][1])
  * **Decision-making on measures/sanctions (explicit):** the President/Presidenta decides the follow-up measures after receiving the référent’s report. ([unistra.fr][3])

* **Promoting academic integrity (explicit):**

  * Promotes and ensures application of the university’s **Charte de déontologie des métiers de la recherche de l’Université de Strasbourg**. ([unistra.fr][1])

* **Overseeing integrity regulations / ordinance (explicitly mentioned elements):**

  * “Promouvoir et faire appliquer” the university research-ethics/deontology charter (as above). ([unistra.fr][1])
  * No explicit statement found (in the consulted official sources) that this unit “oversees” a broader integrity ordinance/regulatory framework beyond the charter and the described signalement/instruction process. ([unistra.fr][1])

5. **Reporting & authority structure**

a. **Reports to**

* **Référent / Référente (intégrité scientifique):** produces **a report addressed to the President of the university**. ([unistra.fr][1])
* **Cellule « Déontologie de la recherche » (as described in the university’s “Guide du chercheur”):** **placed under the responsibility of the Vice-President “Recherche et Formation doctorale”**; in case of disagreement between parties, the matter can be escalated to the **President of the university**. ([documentation.unistra.fr][2])

b. **Decision-making authority**

* **Référent / Référente:**

  * **Has procedural authority** to decide whether to open an “instruction” (“décide s’il y a lieu d’ouvrir une instruction”). ([unistra.fr][1])
  * **Final decision authority on outcomes is explicitly with the President** (“C’est le Président qui décide des suites… (sanctions, réhabilitations, etc.)”). ([unistra.fr][1])
  * **Classification (explicitly supported):** **Mixed** (référent decides on opening an instruction; President decides outcomes). ([unistra.fr][1])

* **Cellule « Déontologie de la recherche » (Guide du chercheur):**

  * Conducts preliminary instruction that can result in **closing without further action** or **continuing instruction**. ([documentation.unistra.fr][2])
  * If there is disagreement, the **President** can order further inquiry or consult a committee; the President can also appoint ad hoc experts. ([documentation.unistra.fr][2])
  * The document states that **“the university takes a decision”** after the investigation (e.g., injunction to end the breach, referral to disciplinary bodies). ([documentation.unistra.fr][2])
  * **Classification:** **Not specified in official sources** in a single clear label (the text describes steps and actors, but does not explicitly label the Cellule as “advisory only” vs “final authority”). ([documentation.unistra.fr][2])

c. **Appeal or second-opinion mechanisms**

* **Appeal:** **Not specified in official sources.** ([unistra.fr][1])
* **Second-opinion / escalation mechanisms (explicitly described):**

  * The President may **order a supplementary inquiry** or **refer the matter to the consultative committee**; the President may appoint **ad hoc expert(s)**. ([documentation.unistra.fr][2])
  * Possible escalation to **disciplinary bodies** (section disciplinaire / CPE) or transmission to the competent disciplinary authority for certain staff categories. ([documentation.unistra.fr][2])

* Université de Strasbourg (documentation.unistra.fr) — *Charte de déontologie des métiers de la recherche* / *Guide du chercheur* (Cellule under VP Research & Doctoral Training; escalation to President; decision options; referral to disciplinary bodies). ([documentation.unistra.fr][2])
