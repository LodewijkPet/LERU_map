I’ll look up Utrecht University’s official pages/regulations on scientific integrity and misconduct, identify the named unit(s) that handle cases and policy, and then summarize only what’s explicitly stated with exact source links.


1. **Official name of the unit**
   **Commissie wetenschappelijke integriteit** (*Committee for Research Integrity*) ([Universiteit Utrecht][1])

2. **Institutional location**

* **Established by / positioned under:** the **College van Bestuur** (Executive Board) establishes the committee and appoints its chair and members. ([Universiteit Utrecht][2])
* **Administrative/legal support:** the committee is **supported by jurists from the afdeling Juridische Zaken (Legal Affairs)** and advises the Executive Board based on its investigation (complaints are submitted via **[secr.jz@uu.nl](mailto:secr.jz@uu.nl)**). ([Universiteit Utrecht][1])

3. **Core responsibilities**

* **Handling academic misconduct cases — explicitly mentioned**

  * Investigates **formal complaints** about suspected breaches of research/scientific integrity. ([Universiteit Utrecht][1])
  * Assesses whether research integrity **has been violated** and **advises** the Executive Board. ([Universiteit Utrecht][2])
  * Can also conduct an investigation **at the request of the Executive Board even without a filed complaint**. ([Universiteit Utrecht][2])

* **Promoting academic integrity — Not specified in official sources.**
  *(Related role in the official procedure: the **vertrouwenspersoon wetenschappelijke integriteit** is described as a low-threshold contact for questions/complaints and may mediate; this is separate from the committee’s complaint investigation role.)* ([Universiteit Utrecht][2])

* **Overseeing integrity regulations / ordinance — Not specified in official sources.** ([Universiteit Utrecht][2])

5. **Reporting & authority structure**

a. **Reports to**

* **College van Bestuur (Executive Board / “Bestuur”)**: the committee *“brengt … advies uit aan het bestuur”* and also provides **annual accountability** to the Board via a yearly report (*“legt … verantwoording af aan het bestuur”*). ([Universiteit Utrecht][1])
* **Exception**: if a complaint concerns a **member of the Executive Board**, the **Raad van Toezicht (Supervisory Board)** exercises the role and powers assigned to the Board in this regulation. ([Universiteit Utrecht][1])

b. **Decision-making authority**

* **Committee (Commissie wetenschappelijke integriteit): advisory** — it investigates, assesses whether integrity was breached, and **issues advice to the Board**. ([Universiteit Utrecht][1])
* **Executive Board (College van Bestuur): decision authority** — it sets the **“aanvankelijk oordeel”** (initial judgement) and later the **“definitieve oordeel”** (final judgement). ([Universiteit Utrecht][1])

c. **Appeal or second-opinion mechanisms**

* **LOWI (Landelijk Orgaan voor Wetenschappelijke Integriteit)**: complainant/defendant/other stakeholders **may request LOWI advice** on the Board’s initial judgement within **six weeks**; if LOWI advice is requested, the Board **takes it into account** in the final judgement. ([Universiteit Utrecht][1])
