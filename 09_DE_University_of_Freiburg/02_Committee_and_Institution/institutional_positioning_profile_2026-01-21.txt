5. **Reporting & authority structure**

a. **Reports to**

* **Koordinierungsstelle „Redlichkeit in der Wissenschaft“:** Not specified as a formal reporting line in official sources. Official university communication states that the coordination office *prepares the Rector’s decisions* in misconduct cases and *informs the Rector and the Rectorate* about ongoing procedures and case numbers. ([kommunikation.uni-freiburg.de][1])
* **Untersuchungskommission zur Sicherung der Redlichkeit in der Wissenschaft:** Advises the Rector and submits a status report on investigations to the Rector; if the Commission finds indications of disciplinary/contractually relevant behaviour, it must notify the Rector. ([University of Freiburg][2])
* **Ombudsperson für die Selbstkontrolle in der Wissenschaft:** Appointed by the Senate on proposal of the Rector; if suspicion is considered sufficiently substantiated after plausibility review, the Ombudsperson informs the “zuständigen Gremien” (competent bodies—no further specification given). ([University of Freiburg][2])

b. **Decision-making authority**

* **Untersuchungskommission:** Investigatory/advisory role only. It is explicitly stated that it is **not authorised to propose sanctions or make recommendations**, and the Rector is **not legally bound** by the status report. ([University of Freiburg][2])
* **Rector (Rektorin/Rektor):** Explicitly stated as the decision-maker on whether (and to whom) the Commission’s status report is disclosed; the coordination office prepares the Rector’s decisions in misconduct cases. ([University of Freiburg][2])
* **Ombudsperson:** Provides independent advice and conducts plausibility review; no sanctioning or final decision authority is stated for the Ombudsperson. ([University of Freiburg][2])

c. **Appeal or second-opinion mechanisms**

* **Second-opinion / external route explicitly mentioned:** Members/affiliates and other informants may contact the university’s Ombudsperson, and persons with a connection to the German research system may alternatively contact the national body **“Ombudsman für die Wissenschaft.”** ([University of Freiburg][2])
* **Internal appeal mechanism:** Not specified in official sources. ([University of Freiburg][2])
